# Idea Evolution · 观念演化

A civilization-scale system modeling how memes, religions, ideologies, paradigms, and narratives spread, mutate, compete, and reshape human history. Bilingual (EN / 中文) with a global language toggle.

The site treats ideas as evolutionary organisms with eight measurable traits — emotional resonance, simplicity, institutional support, technological amplification, adaptability, social utility, narrative power, and symbolic compression — and visualizes their propagation across geography and time.

## Live systems

- **Global Idea Network** — interactive world map of origins and spread arcs for 30+ ideas across six categories (religion, philosophy, ideology, paradigm, meme, symbol).
- **Memetic Lineage** — a force-directed graph laid out on a year axis, showing which ideas descend from which.
- **Religion Propagation** — a year slider that animates Buddhism, Christianity, Islam, Confucianism, Hinduism, and Judaism radiating across centuries.
- **Idea Survival Score** — a radar plot of the eight traits for any selected idea.
- **Ask the System** — an analytical AI voice (memetics researcher / historian / network scientist) that connects ideas across eras.
